Organizational Forms of Businesses
- Sole proprietorship
- General partnership
- Limited partnership
- Corporation

Key Questions:
- Legal entity
- Management
- Liability
- Tax status
- Access to capital

Organizational Forms of Businesses
Sole proprietorship: owned and operated by an individual
- Sole claim to profits
- Personally liable for claims against the business
- Profits taxed as personal income


Organizational Forms of Businesses
Limited partnership: general and limited partners
- General partner(s) operate the business and are personally liable for claims against the business
- Limited partners only liable for amount they invest
- Partnership agreement states claims to profits
- Profits taxed as personal income

Organizational Forms of Businesses
Corporation: legal identity separate from owners
- Owners appoint managers to operate business
- Owners only liable for amount they invest
- Profits may be taxed at corporate level
- Dividends distributed to owners may be taxed as personal income ("double taxation" if corporation is also taxed)
