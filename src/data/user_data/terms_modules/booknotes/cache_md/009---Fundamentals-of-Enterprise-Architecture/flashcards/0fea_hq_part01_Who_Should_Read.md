#### Enterprise Architecture Overview
Enterprise architecture (EA) is a strategic framework that aligns business strategies, processes, and technologies to achieve organizational goals. It provides a structured approach to managing and optimizing an organization's technology landscape, ensuring scalability, flexibility, and coherence in the face of rapid technological change. The concept emerged in the 1980s but has evolved to remain relevant in modern digital transformation initiatives.
