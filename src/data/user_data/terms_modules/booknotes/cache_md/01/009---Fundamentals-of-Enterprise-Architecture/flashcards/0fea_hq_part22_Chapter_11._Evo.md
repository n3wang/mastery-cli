#### Evolution over Frameworks Principle
The "evolution over frameworks" principle emphasizes that while frameworks are useful tools for accelerating development and standardizing processes, the ability to adapt and evolve is more valuable in a rapidly changing technological landscape. This principle is central to effective enterprise architecture, especially in the context of digital transformation and agility.

:p What is the core idea behind the "evolution over frameworks" principle in enterprise architecture?
??x
The core idea is that although frameworks—like TOGAF, Zachman, or DoDAF—are valuable for providing structure, standardization, and reuse, they must not become rigid constraints. In a world where technology and business needs change rapidly, enterprise architecture must evolve to stay relevant. Evolution allows organizations to adapt to new conditions, integrate new technologies (e.g., cloud computing), and maintain alignment with business goals. The ability to tailor and adjust frameworks to fit organizational context is more important than blindly following them.
