The enterprise architect role is a senior-level position focused on making strategic decisions that impact the entire enterprise. Enterprise architects must possess a combination of technical expertise, strategic thinking, and strong communication skills to influence change across technology, process, and culture. They are responsible for defining long-term strategies, establishing technology standards, and aligning architecture with business outcomes.

??x
