#### Case Practice Overview
Background context explaining the importance of case practice. This technique helps build a robust archive in your mind, allowing you to draw from various cases and apply knowledge flexibly.

- **Live practice** vs. reading cases: While live practice is ideal, it can be challenging to find opportunities. Reading as many cases as possible serves as an excellent alternative.
- **Building an Archive**: The more cases you read, the better equipped you are with diverse scenarios and solutions, making it easier to tackle new problems.
- **Drawing from Cases**: When faced with a new case or problem, you can reference past cases to find similar situations and solutions. This helps in structuring your thoughts and approach.

#### Case Practice Structure
This section explains how to use case practice effectively by outlining a structured approach.

- **Read the Case**: Start by thoroughly reading the provided case. Take notes on the details that are relevant but might not be immediately obvious.
- **Write Your Structure**: After reading, jot down your initial thoughts and structure for answering the question or problem presented in the case.
- **Review Notes**: Reread the case after writing your initial response to pick up more details and insights.

#### Case Practice Second Read
This part emphasizes the importance of a second reading after initial thoughts have been written down.

- **Second Reading**: After writing your structure and response, go back through the case again. You will likely pick up more information during this second read than you did the first time.
- **Note Additional Insights**: During the second reading, make notes in your journal of any additional details or insights that come to mind.

:p What is the purpose of a second reading?

The purpose of a second reading is to enhance comprehension by picking up more information and insights during the re-read than you initially noted.
