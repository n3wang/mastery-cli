#### Stuck Again
Background context: If changing wants and offers doesn't resolve the impasse, it's time to shift gears by considering whether there might be deeper issues in how you're working with the client or a problem with the relationship.

:p What should you do if you realize that your attempts at changing wants and offers have led to another impasse?
??x
If you find yourself stuck again after trying different wants and offers, it's important to reassess the dynamics of the relationship between you and the client. Ask yourself whether there might be underlying issues affecting the negotiation process.

You can shift the focus by directly stating that the meeting is not making progress or by asking how the client feels about the meeting:

1. "I think we are stuck."
2. "How do you feel we are doing in reaching agreement on how to proceed?"

These statements help open up a dialogue where both parties can express their concerns and feelings, leading to more productive discussions.

It's crucial to remain non-judgmental during these conversations; simply focus on the fact that the current approach is not yielding results. By addressing the process, you may uncover issues such as trust, control, or misunderstanding that are hindering agreement.
