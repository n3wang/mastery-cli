#### Ivy Case System
The Ivy Case System is a structured approach to solving case questions. It involves several steps that help break down the problem systematically, ensuring a clear path to a solution.

:p What are the first five steps of the Best Case Thinking approach?
??x
The first five steps of the Best Case Thinking approach in the Ivy Case System are:
1. Clarify the problem (What is the core issue? What does success look like?)
2. Develop your structure (How will you organize the information to solve the problem?)
3. Collect data (What key data do you need, and where can you find it?)
4. Calculate (Perform necessary calculations or analysis)
5. Conclude (Draw a clear conclusion based on your findings)
