#### Steps to Resolve Conflict Over Alternatives
To resolve conflicts regarding alternatives effectively, follow a structured process that includes identifying decision owners, gathering stakeholders, listing the alternatives, seeking compromises, creating new alternatives if necessary, and allowing the final decision.

:p What are the six steps to deal with conflict over alternatives?
??x
The six steps to deal with conflict over alternatives include:
1. Establish who owns the decision and will be the final decision maker.
2. Assemble the key stakeholders and reconfirm agreement over the goals and objectives (which are not the conflict in this case).
3. List the alternatives with their advantages and risks, determining if any clearly outscore others when looked at objectively.
4. Seek to combine and compromise on the alternatives to capture the best traits and avoid the worst traits of each.
5. Where necessary, create a new alternative that everyone can live with.
6. Allow the decision owner to make the final decision.

#### Conflict Resolution and Decision Making

Background context: In scenarios where conflict arises within a group, especially when it concerns organizational goals or objectives, effective decision making requires clear differentiation between musts (critical elements) and wants (desirable but non-essential aspects). The goal is to ensure that all parties focus on what is critical for the mission's success while finding reasonable compromises for less essential aspects.

:p How does one distinguish between musts and wants in conflict resolution?
??x
Musts are critical elements that are absolutely necessary for the success of a project or decision. They can be identified through their measurability, realism, and criticality to achieving goals. Wants, on the other hand, are desirable but not essential. High-importance wants often represent the inverse of musts. For instance, if your must is “not to spend more than $10,000,” a want might be “to spend as little as possible.”

The key here is to prioritize and differentiate between these two categories to ensure that the most critical elements are preserved while making reasonable compromises on less essential ones.

```java
// Example of identifying musts and wants in Java code
public class NegotiationExample {
    public void identifyMustsAndWants() {
        String[] musts = {"not to spend more than $10,000", "availability within two weeks"};
        String[] wants = {"to spend as little as possible", "availability sooner if feasible"};
        
        for (String must : musts) {
            System.out.println("Must: " + must);
        }
        
        for (String want : wants) {
            System.out.println("Want: " + want);
        }
    }
}
```

#### Performance Evaluation
Performance should be evaluated quarterly, involving both employee and supervisor discussions on accomplishments, errors, needs, and desires. Annual performance reviews alone by the superior are inadequate for strategic decision-making.

The approach emphasizes continuous feedback and alignment between employee and manager on development needs.
:p How should performance evaluations be conducted?
??x
Performance evaluations should be conducted quarterly, with regular discussions between employees and their supervisors. These sessions focus on discussing accomplishments, identifying errors, addressing needs, and expressing desires. The process ensures ongoing feedback and aligns both parties on the development plans required for future roles.
