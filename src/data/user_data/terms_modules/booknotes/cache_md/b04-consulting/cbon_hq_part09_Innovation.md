#### Professional Integrity
Background context explaining the importance of walking the talk—meaning that if you advise clients on behavior, you should model that behavior yourself. Providing an example from the text about advising a client to act ethically while also practicing ethical behavior yourself.
:p What does "walk the talk" mean in the context of professional advice?
??x
"Walk the talk" refers to the principle that professionals should exemplify the behavior they advise their clients to adopt. This means that if you counsel your clients on how to behave, especially when it comes to matters such as ethics and integrity, you must also demonstrate these qualities yourself.

For example, if you are advising a business owner of a gym to act ethically regarding financial reporting or client interactions, you should ensure that you adhere to the same principles. This includes being transparent, honest, and fair in all your dealings.

From the text: "If you're going to advise your client on how to act, then you have to know how to act." This statement underscores the importance of professional integrity.

Here’s an example:
- If you advise a gym owner that they should provide accurate billing information, you must ensure that your own financial records are also accurate and transparent.
