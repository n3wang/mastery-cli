#### Determining ROI from Performance Consulting Initiatives is Possible

Background context: Understanding return on investment (ROI) has become increasingly important for clients when evaluating performance consulting initiatives. This involves quantifying the benefits derived from such initiatives relative to their costs.

Relevant formulas and explanations:
- **BCR (Benefit/Cost Ratio):** $$\text{BCR} = \frac{\text{Monetary value of benefits}}{\text{Total cost of initiative}}$$
- **ROI as a percentage:** $$\text{ROI (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Net benefits}}{\text{Cost of investment}} \right) \times 100$$

:p How can the benefit/cost ratio be calculated?
??x
The benefit/cost ratio (BCR) is calculated by dividing the monetary value of the benefits derived from the performance consulting initiative by the total cost incurred to design, develop, and implement the initiative. This gives an indication of whether the investment was worthwhile.
$$\text{BCR} = \frac{\text{Monetary value of benefits}}{\text{Total cost of initiative}}$$

:p How is ROI as a percentage calculated?
??x
ROI (Return on Investment) as a percentage is calculated by dividing the net benefits by the cost of investment and multiplying by 100. Net benefits are the total monetary value of the benefits minus any costs associated with maintaining or enhancing these benefits.
$$\text{ROI (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Net benefits}}{\text{Cost of investment}} \right) \times 100$$
