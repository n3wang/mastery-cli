#### Interviewing Star Employees
The challenge when interviewing employees is to ask questions that prompt them to explain their behaviors and actions leading to successful outcomes. Since much of what star employees do is at an unconscious competence level, it's crucial to probe deeply into the specific steps they take.

:p What are some key techniques for conducting interviews with star employees?
??x
Some key techniques include:

1. **Preparation**: Send pre-work to the employee identifying the business needs or performance accomplishments you will focus on.
2. **Starter Questions**: Use starter questions like:
   - "In this discussion, I would like to first discuss the business goal [or accomplishment] of __________."
   - "I would like to learn what you specifically do to accomplish this result."

3. **Deep Probing**: Ask detailed follow-up questions such as: 
   - "What practices do you use to develop trust with a customer?"
4. **Problem Identification and Solutions**: Ask about problems encountered and how they are overcome.
5. **Criteria for Success**: Determine the criteria used by the employee to judge success.
6. **Organizational Barriers and Enablers**: Identify both organizational barriers that hinder performance and enablers that support it.

By using these techniques, you can gain deeper insights into the actions and behaviors of star employees, which are crucial for creating effective models or practices.

#### Identifying Steps and Actions
To get detailed information from star employees, it's crucial to ask them to walk through their steps and actions in achieving specific results. This involves probing beyond surface-level behaviors to uncover underlying practices.

:p What technique can be used to ensure that deep insights are gained during the interview?
??x
A useful technique is to organize the discussion into a sequence of steps or actions. You might ask questions like:

- "Walk me through the steps and actions you take to achieve [specific result]."
- "Please describe what you do first, next, and so on."

This method works well when discussing processes integrated into specific results, such as project management.

For example:
- For a revenue goal: "Walk me through the steps and actions you take to achieve the revenue goal expected of you."
- For project management: "Please describe the actions you take to ensure your employees are capable to perform their jobs as needed."

By breaking down the process into steps, you can probe deeper into the specific behaviors and decisions taken by the star employee.

#### Criteria for Success
Understanding the criteria used by star employees to judge success is crucial. This involves both quantitative and qualitative measures.

:p How do you determine the criteria for measuring success in a project or task?
??x
You can ask:
"How do you know that you have accomplished this result in an excellent manner? What criteria do you use to determine that the result was completed successfully?"

To ensure comprehensive understanding, seek out both quantitative and qualitative criteria.

For example:
- Quantitative: "What specific metrics or numbers indicate success?" (e.g., revenue targets met)
- Qualitative: "How do customers or colleagues acknowledge the value of your work?" (e.g., customer testimonials)

By identifying these criteria, you can create a more holistic model that reflects both performance and quality.

#### Identifying Organizational Barriers
Identifying barriers to organizational success is crucial for understanding what needs to be improved. This involves asking about obstacles encountered by star employees in their roles.

:p How do you identify organizational barriers during an interview with a star employee?
??x
You can use the following question:
"What obstacles or barriers within our organization, if any, do you encounter as you work in support of this result?"

This question helps uncover both internal and external factors that hinder performance. It is important to explore:

- **Support from Subject Matter Experts**: Are there sufficient experts available?
- **Database Issues**: Are databases up-to-date?
- **Skill Gaps**: Are necessary skills and knowledge present?

By identifying these barriers, you can work towards removing them or finding solutions.

#### Identifying Organizational Enablers
Understanding the organizational factors that support star employees is crucial for sustaining performance. This involves asking about supportive elements within the organization.

:p How do you identify organizational enablers during an interview with a star employee?
??x
You can use the following question:
"What factors within our organization are supporting you as you work to achieve this result?"

This question helps identify:

- **Supportive Resources**: Are there adequate resources or tools available?
- **Leadership Support**: Does leadership provide necessary support and encouragement?
- **Training and Development**: Is ongoing training and development provided?

By identifying these enablers, you can understand what is working well and potentially replicate this success across the organization.
