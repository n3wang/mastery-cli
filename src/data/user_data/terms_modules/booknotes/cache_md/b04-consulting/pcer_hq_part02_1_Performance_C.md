#### Identifying True Clients
The concept revolves around understanding who the true client is within an organization, as it significantly impacts the success of a performance consulting initiative. A client owns and is accountable for achieving business results, has authority to make decisions, and is within the chain of command affecting employee performance.

Clients can be categorized into two types:
- **Sustained Clients**: These are individuals who maintain a continuous partnership with you and have significant influence over business outcomes. They typically hold high-level positions such as president, CEO, vice presidents, or directors.
- **Project Clients**: These clients are involved specifically for the duration of a project but may not have the same level of ongoing influence.

The importance of identifying these clients early in the process cannot be overstated to ensure effective communication and support. Failure to recognize true clients can lead to delays or failure in initiatives.
:p What is a key characteristic that distinguishes sustained clients from project clients?
??x
Sustained clients have a continuous partnership with you, are typically located in mid- to upper management levels (e.g., president, CEO, vice presidents, directors), and maintain authority over business results. Project clients, on the other hand, are involved for specific projects but may not require as much ongoing communication.

#### Client vs Stakeholder
This concept highlights the difference between a client and a stakeholder in a performance consulting initiative. Clients own and are accountable for achieving business results, while stakeholders have an interest in the outcome but do not carry full accountability.

For example:
- **Client**: The vice president of sales owns the goal to achieve increased revenue.
- **Stakeholders**: First-line supervisors who manage direct reports' performance play a crucial role by supporting or hindering the initiative's success within their span of control.

Engaging stakeholders is essential as they can significantly influence the outcome, either positively or negatively. Effective strategies must be developed to involve these individuals in the process.
:p How do clients and stakeholders differ in terms of accountability?
??x
Clients are accountable for achieving the overall business results addressed by a performance consulting initiative (e.g., sales goals, profit margins). In contrast, stakeholders may have a vested interest or influence on the outcome but do not carry full responsibility. For instance, first-line supervisors are accountable only within their span of control.
