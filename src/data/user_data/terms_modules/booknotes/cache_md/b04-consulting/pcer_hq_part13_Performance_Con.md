#### A SHOULD Assessment
A SHOULD assessment is a comprehensive evaluation designed to determine the desired state for (a) the business, (b) people’s performance, and (c) the organization. This process supports needed performance by identifying areas that require improvement or enhancement.

:p What are the main purposes of a SHOULD assessment?
??x
The main purposes of a SHOULD assessment include determining the desired state of the business, identifying optimal performance levels for employees, and establishing organizational benchmarks to support necessary improvements in both processes and human capabilities. This holistic approach ensures that all aspects contributing to successful performance are considered.

---

#### Common Outputs of a SHOULD Assessment Focusing on Employee Performance
The most frequent outputs from a SHOULD assessment focused on employee performance include process models, competency models, and performance models. These models serve as blueprints for understanding how work is performed, the skills required, and the outcomes expected.

:p What are the common outputs of a SHOULD assessment when focusing on employee performance?
??x
The common outputs include:
- Process Models: Describe the steps involved in performing tasks or achieving goals.
- Competency Models: Define the skills, knowledge, and behaviors required for successful job performance.
- Performance Models: Outline expected outcomes or standards for performance.

---

#### Determining the Optimal Model for a Situation
To determine which model is optimal for a situation, it’s crucial to clarify with the client how the information will be used. The applications intended from the information will guide the choice of the most suitable type of model.

:p How do you decide which model is best when conducting a SHOULD assessment?
??x
Deciding on the best model involves understanding the specific needs and goals of the organization. By consulting with clients to identify how the models will be used, you can choose among process models (for detailed procedures), competency models (to focus on required skills), or performance models (to set outcome benchmarks).

#### Utilizing Star Employees
Star employees are a critical source of information when building models because they demonstrate exceptional results through their day-to-day performance. Their insights provide valuable data and best practices.

:p How can star employees contribute to the development of performance and competency models?
??x
Star employees can significantly enhance model development by offering firsthand experiences and successful strategies. Their achievements highlight effective processes, key competencies, and high-performance standards that can be used as benchmarks for other employees or organizational units.

#### Absence of Metrics: Defining Success
Background context explaining the concept. Sometimes, the organization may have goals but lacks metrics for them. In these scenarios, it is essential to work with the client to define acceptable measures that can be used to determine the current state (IS).
:p How should you handle a goal like increasing positive comments on social media when no metric exists?
??x
Work with the client to define what success looks like in this context. For instance, if the goal is to increase positive comments on social media, discuss how many comments are considered "positive" and agree on a measure that can be used to quantify this. This could involve creating a rating system or defining criteria for positivity.
