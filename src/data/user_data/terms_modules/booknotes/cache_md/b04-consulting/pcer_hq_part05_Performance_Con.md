#### Performance Consulting Mental Models
Performance consultants use mental models to analyze business and workgroup performance situations. These models guide their approach and involve aligning four needs: business, performance, organizational capability, and individual capability.

The Need Hierarchy is a critical component of these mental models. It consists of four levels:
1. **Business Needs**: Focused on the overall goals and objectives that the organization aims to achieve.
2. **Performance Needs**: Centered around achieving optimal operational efficiency and productivity.
3. **Organizational Capability Needs**: Addressing the systems, processes, and structures required for performance.
4. **Individual Capability Needs**: Concentrating on the skills, knowledge, and competencies of individuals.

#### Gaps Map Tool
The Gaps Map is a tool designed by performance consultants to assist in obtaining information needed to address a specific business need. It involves mapping out the differences between what should be (SHOULD) and what currently is (IS), as well as identifying the root causes (CAUSE).

:p What is the purpose of the Gaps Map?

#### Identify Strategic Opportunities Reactively: Reframing Requests

When clients approach with specific requests to implement solutions, it’s crucial to shift focus from the proposed solution to the underlying business and performance outcomes they seek.

Background context: In B2C environments, client requests can often be too narrow in scope. By reframing the discussion, you can align the proposed solution more closely with the client's broader goals, ensuring that the outcome is meaningful and beneficial for their organization.

:p How does rephrasing client requests help in achieving strategic opportunities?
??x
Rephrasing client requests helps by focusing on the business and performance outcomes rather than the specific solutions. This approach ensures that the proposed strategies align more closely with the client's broader goals, making the solution more effective and aligned with their priorities.

For example, if a client asks for an e-commerce platform implementation, you can reframe it as understanding how to optimize customer engagement and revenue growth through digital channels ($R = P \cdot Q$, where \( R \) is Revenue, \( P \) is Price, and \( Q \) is Quantity). This reframing allows you to explore various solutions that may better address the client's objectives.

Background context: Performance consultants engage in three types of work—transactional, tactical, and strategic. Each type has a different level of impact on organizational health and direction.
