#### CAUSE Analysis
CAUSE analysis is a method for identifying root causes of performance gaps. The categories include:
1. Factors External to the Organization
2. Internal Organizational Capability
3. Internal Individual Factors

The formula for analyzing these categories involves sorting and sifting through barriers and enablers within each category.
$$
\text{CAUSE Analysis} = \sum (\text{Barriers} + \text{Enablers}) - \text{Irrelevant Factors}
$$

:p What is CAUSE analysis, and how does it work?
??x
CAUSE analysis is a method for identifying the root causes of performance gaps. It involves categorizing factors into three main groups:
1. Factors external to the organization: such as competitive pressures, economic conditions, weather, and regulatory requirements.
2. Internal organizational capability: including culture, strategy, systems, processes, and technology.
3. Internal individual factors: like skills, knowledge, attitudes, behaviors, work environment, and support.

The goal is to determine which categories are creating barriers (hindering performance), enabling (supporting performance), or having no impact in a given situation. This helps prioritize where solutions should be implemented.

For example, if "information necessary to perform" is available but employees do not have access, the issue lies within individual factors rather than external or organizational capability.
