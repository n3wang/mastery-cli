??x
Some common forms of resistance include:
- **Emotional Reactions**: Clients might express frustration or anger due to the realization of difficult organizational problems.
- **Defensiveness**: Clients may become defensive when presented with challenging data or recommendations that highlight their shortcomings.
- **Passive Resistance**: This can manifest as reluctance to engage fully in discussions, leading to incomplete meetings or tasks.
