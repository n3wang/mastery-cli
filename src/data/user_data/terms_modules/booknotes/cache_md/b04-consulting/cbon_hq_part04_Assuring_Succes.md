#### Find the Economic Buyer
Background context: The first step to ensure a successful proposal is identifying the economic buyer, which is often not done diligently by consultants. Focusing on low-level support areas instead of the true buyer can lead to rejection and loss of business.

:p What are the key steps in finding the economic buyer?
??x
To find the economic buyer, you must ask questions that will help identify who has the budgetary approval and can make a decision. This involves dealing with low-level people only as long as they can introduce or identify the true buyer. The objective is to avoid spending time on unthreatening but non-decision-making levels.

You should establish yourself as a peer of the true buyer by wearing good clothing, demonstrating value, and providing examples of how you've helped other firms. This helps build trust and ensures that your proposal has a higher chance of acceptance.

#### Measures of Success (Metrics)
Background context: Metrics provide a way to track progress towards achieving goals. Both factual and subjective metrics should be used effectively to demonstrate the impact of proposed solutions.

:p What are some examples of lousy indicators?
??x
Lousy indicators include vague statements such as "The workforce will be better informed" or "Customers will have greater loyalty." These do not provide clear, observable evidence of progress. Better examples would include specific metrics like reducing call transfers by 10% or increasing repeat business.

#### Measures of Success
Measures of success are outlined similarly to objectives, ensuring quantifiable outcomes that can be tracked during project execution.

:p How should measures of success be formatted?
??x
Measures of success should also be presented in bullet points. Each measure should be specific and measurable. For example:

- Monthly sales reports indicate growing percentage of foreign sales.
- No net increases in employment on monthly payroll.
- Net revenue monthly reports increase over prior months.

These points help in tracking progress towards the set objectives and provide clear benchmarks for success.
