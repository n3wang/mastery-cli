#### Identifying Resistance Cues
Background context: The ability to identify and understand resistance is crucial for effective consultation. Resistance often manifests as indirect expressions of client reservations, which can be subtle or overt. Recognizing these cues helps consultants understand the real issues at hand.

:p How do you pick up the cues from a client's behavior to recognize resistance?

??x
To pick up the cues, focus on nonverbal messages such as physical movements (e.g., moving away, crossing arms), facial expressions, and body language. Pay attention if the client is tied up in knots, pointing fingers, shaking their head while speaking, or leaning toward you aggressively. These behaviors indicate discomfort with the project.

Additionally, listen to your own reactions: feeling uneasy, boredom, or irritation often signal that resistance is present. Notice any physical signs like yawning or suppression of negative feelings as they act as red flags and attention-getting devices.
