#### Valid Consideration in Consulting Agreements
Background context explaining the importance of valid consideration for solid contracts. This concept is crucial to ensure both parties benefit equitably from consulting engagements.

For our purposes, **consideration** is defined as an exchange of something of value between the consultant and the client. Internal consultants often focus on providing advice, analysis, or reflection (service) to their line person counterparts without acknowledging the need for reciprocal benefits. A valid contract requires mutual consideration where the support person also receives tangible benefits.

Support people often emphasize appreciation as a form of consideration. While emotional satisfaction can be significant, there are more tangible items that should be part of the original agreement:

- **Operational Partnership**: Influence on decision-making, access to critical events and information.
- **Access to People and Information**: Freedom to engage with relevant stakeholders and gather necessary data without restrictions.
- **Time of People in the Line Organization**: Time is a significant cost for improvement projects. Consultants need sufficient time from line organization members to plan and implement changes.
- **Opportunity to Be Innovative**: The freedom to experiment and try new approaches.

These elements ensure that consultants are not coerced into agreements where they might feel their contributions are undervalued or ignored, leading to dissatisfaction later.

:p What is the concept of valid consideration in consulting agreements?
??x
Valid consideration refers to an exchange of something of value between a consultant and a client. It ensures both parties benefit from the engagement. For support personnel (internal consultants), valid consideration includes:
- Operational partnership: Influence on decision-making processes.
- Access to people and information: Freedom to engage with relevant stakeholders.
- Time allocation: Sufficient time from line organization members for planning and implementation.
- Opportunity for innovation: The freedom to try new approaches.

Without valid consideration, the agreement may become inequitable and can lead to dissatisfaction when results do not meet expectations. This concept is crucial in ensuring that both parties are adequately compensated and motivated throughout the project.
