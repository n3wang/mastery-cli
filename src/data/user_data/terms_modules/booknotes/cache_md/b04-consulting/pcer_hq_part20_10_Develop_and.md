#### Collect Data at All Levels
In order to connect business results to solutions and diagnose reasons for less-than-satisfactory outcomes, it is essential to collect data at all levels of evaluation. This approach ensures that you can trace back from high-level business impacts (Level 4) down to the foundational learning and performance metrics (Levels 1 through 3).

By obtaining information on Levels 1 (Reaction), 2 (Organizational and Individual Capability), and 3 (Performance) when planning for Level 4 (Business) data, you establish a comprehensive understanding of the impact of your initiatives.

:p What is the importance of collecting data at all levels?
??x
