#### Identifying Strategic Opportunities
The first phase of the Performance Consulting Process involves identifying opportunities where performance consultants can partner with their clients. These opportunities are often identified reactively or proactively.

- **Reactive Identification:** This occurs when a client requests a specific solution and aims to reframe the request into focusing on desired results rather than the proposed solution.
- **Proactive Identification:** Initiated by you, this approach involves discussing future goals with your clients. The goal is to raise awareness about factors that may contribute to business results but are not currently on the radar.

:p What is a key difference between reactive and proactive identification of opportunities in performance consulting?
??x
Reactive identification involves responding to client requests for specific solutions, while proactive identification focuses on initiating discussions with clients about future goals and potential factors contributing to business success that may not be immediately apparent.
x??

#### Assessing the Strategic Nature of the Opportunity
Once an opportunity is identified, it needs to be assessed to determine if it should be managed strategically or tactically. A strategic situation must meet certain criteria:

1. **Business Needs:** The initiative directly supports one or more business needs.
2. **Client Access:** You have direct access to clients accountable for achieving the business needs.
3. **Performance Change:** The client is seeking performance change from people in one or more employee groups.
4. **Accountability Sharing:** The client is willing to share accountability with you for producing the required change.
5. **Information Availability:** The client will provide necessary information and time to obtain that information before deciding on and implementing solutions.
6. **Group Focus:** The situation focuses on a group of people rather than individuals.

:p How many criteria must be met for an opportunity to qualify as a strategic opportunity?
??x
There are six criteria that must be met for an opportunity to qualify as a strategic opportunity in performance consulting.
x??

#### Strategic Assessments

Background context explaining the concept. There are three types of strategic assessments: SHOULD, IS, and CAUSE. Each serves a different purpose in determining the desired state, current state, and root causes for gaps in business and performance results.

:p What is the goal of SHOULD assessments?
??x
The goal of SHOULD assessments is to determine, in specific terms, what operational results are required of the business and what people need to do day-to-day if these results are to be achieved. Essentially, you are identifying the desired state for both the business and the performance of people who support that business.
x??

#### IS Assessments

Background context explaining the concept. An IS assessment focuses on identifying the current operational results of the business and the actions employees currently take to support those goals.

:p What does an IS assessment aim to identify?
??x
An IS assessment aims to identify the current state for both the business and employees, including:
- The current operational results for the business.
- What employees are currently doing on the job to support these goals.
- What they are not doing.
This information helps in identifying the gap that exists between the desired and current state.
x??

#### CAUSE Assessments

Background context explaining the concept. A CAUSE assessment focuses on determining the root causes for gaps in business and performance results, ensuring actions taken are effective by addressing underlying issues rather than just symptoms.

??x
The primary goal of a CAUSE assessment is to identify the root causes of gaps in business and performance results. It's vital to focus on underlying issues (root causes) instead of superficial symptoms when taking actions to close these gaps.

#### Performance Consulting Process

The Performance Consulting Process is a four-phased workflow that aims to maximize the performance of people and organizations.

:p What does the Performance Consulting Process consist of?
??x
The Performance Consulting Process consists of four phases:
1. **Discovery**: Identifying strategic needs and aligning them with business goals.
2. **Analysis**: Assessing current performance gaps and understanding their root causes.
3. **Design**: Developing solutions to address identified issues.
4. **Implementation**: Putting the designed solutions into practice.

Each phase is critical for achieving effective performance improvement in organizations.

#### Need Hierarchy in Performance Consulting
Background context explaining the concept. The need hierarchy refers to understanding and addressing the root causes of problems before implementing solutions. In performance consulting, this involves identifying the underlying issues that affect project management success.
:p What is the role of the need hierarchy in performance consulting?
??x

#### SHOULD-IS-CAUSE Logic in Performance Consulting
Background context explaining the concept. The SHOULD-IS-CAUSE logic is a framework for understanding and shaping organizational behavior by defining goals (SHOULD), identifying current states (IS), and finding causal relationships that connect the two.
:p How does the SHOULD-IS-CAUSE logic work in performance consulting?
??x
The SHOULD-IS-CAUSE logic helps consultants identify gaps between desired outcomes and current realities. By defining what should be achieved (SHOULD) and understanding the current state (IS), consultants can then find causal relationships that explain why there is a gap. For example, to improve project completion rates:
- SHOULD: 95% of projects completed on time
- IS: Only 58% of projects meet this goal
- CAUSE: Delays are due to inadequate resource allocation and overtime work.
x??
