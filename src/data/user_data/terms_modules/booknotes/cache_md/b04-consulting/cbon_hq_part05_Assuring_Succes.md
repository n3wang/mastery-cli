#### Objectives
Objectives are business outcomes rather than tasks or deliverables. A non-buyer will often ask about specific tasks, deadlines, and deliverable appearances instead of focusing on outcomes. Examples include improving time to market, reducing involuntary turnover, and lowering absenteeism.

:p What distinguishes objectives from tasks or deliverables?
??x
Objectives are distinct from tasks or deliverables in that they focus on business outcomes rather than the processes or products involved. For instance, instead of asking about specific tasks like "how many days?" or "what do the materials look like?", a non-buyer might inquire about the broader impact such as reducing involuntary turnover and lowering absenteeism.

#### Measures of Success
Measures of success, or metrics, are indicators that show progress toward achieving goals. They can be either factual and objective or subjective with acknowledged sources. Poor metrics include vague statements like "the workforce will be better informed" because they lack clear evidence of change.

:p What types of measures of success are described in the text?
??x
Measures of success, or metrics, can be categorized into two types:
1. **Factual and Objective**: Examples include service representatives forwarding fewer than 10 percent of calls to technical experts, repeat business growing over six months, managers reducing their average workweek through delegation, and fewer grievances about privacy violations.
2. **Subjective (Anecdotal) with Acknowledged Sources**: These involve reports from key stakeholders such as the buyer, sales vice president, or R&D director.

#### The Gospel of Value
Value is fundamental to establishing a return on investment (ROI). Without proving the value of the results to the buyer, it's unwise to proceed with a proposal. This involves demonstrating how your work contributes to specific business outcomes.

:p What does the text emphasize about establishing value?

The text emphasizes that value must be clearly established with the buyer before moving forward with any proposal. Demonstrating how your work directly contributes to tangible business outcomes is crucial for gaining acceptance and justifying investment.

#### Measures of Success
Measures of success define how the objectives will be evaluated. They are crucial to ensure that both parties understand and agree on what constitutes success.

:p How do you format measures of success in a proposal?
??x
Format measures of success similarly to objectives, using bullet points:
- "Monthly sales reports indicate growing percentage of foreign sales."
- "No net increases in employment on monthly payroll."
- "Net revenue monthly reports increase over prior months."

This ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the criteria for measuring progress.
