#### Building Trust Through Actions
Explanation of the various ways to build trust. This includes maintaining confidences, avoiding mixed messages, supporting organizational values, addressing issues directly, being a straight-shooter, acting as a sounding board, acknowledging others' views, sharing different opinions, focusing on shared goals, and taking accountability for actions.
:p How can you ensure congruence between your words and actions to build trust?
??x
To ensure congruence between words and actions, maintain consistency in what you say and do. This involves avoiding mixed messages, acting in line with organizational values, directly addressing issues with clients rather than going through others first, being a straight-shooter by discussing issues openly, acting as a sounding board on sensitive topics, acknowledging other people's points of view, sharing your own opinions even when they differ from the majority, and focusing on shared goals.

#### Credibility :p What does credibility imply in performance consulting?
??x
Credibility in performance consulting implies having deep knowledge of the business and industry. This includes understanding various potential solutions that can address specific challenges or opportunities. It also involves staying informed about new trends, technologies, and methodologies relevant to your field.

For example:
- Conduct regular research on industry best practices.
- Continuously update your skills and certifications related to performance consulting.
- Engage in networking with peers and experts within the industry.

By maintaining high credibility, you ensure that your insights and recommendations are well-informed and valuable, thus gaining trust from clients.
