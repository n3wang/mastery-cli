#### Expert Role

Background context: In an expert role, consultants act as experts providing guidance and solutions to problems identified by clients. This can involve tasks like diagnosing issues or suggesting improvements.

??x

An organization’s new corporate benefits strategy has been completely overhauled, and it is clear that the website and online delivery have not met expectations.

The manager calls in a staff website design specialist and says: "I have neither the time nor the inclination to deal with this problem. You're the expert; find out what's wrong and fix it."

Here, the consultant has a free hand to examine the whole system and do whatever analysis and fixing is necessary.

#### Pair-of-Hands Role

Background context: In the pair-of-hands role, consultants assist directly by performing tasks alongside clients or employees. This can be useful when hands-on involvement is required.

:p How does the pair-of-hands role differ from the expert role?
??x
The pair-of-hands role differs from the expert role in that it involves direct participation and physical assistance rather than just providing advice and solutions. While experts are typically involved in diagnosing issues and suggesting improvements, pair-of-hands consultants take a more active hands-on approach.

For example, if a new corporate benefits strategy requires changes to the website's design, the manager might call in both an expert (for diagnosis) and a team member or consultant willing to work side-by-side with the staff to implement these changes.

#### Collaborative Role

Background context: In the collaborative role, consultants work closely with clients, sharing ideas and jointly developing solutions. This approach emphasizes mutual trust and shared decision-making.

This approach helps ensure that the client feels involved and valued, leading to more sustainable and effective outcomes.

#### Collaborative Role Definition
The collaborative role in management emphasizes active involvement of both consultants and managers in various stages of problem-solving, including data gathering and analysis, goal setting, action planning, and sharing responsibility. This approach fosters an interdependent relationship where mutual exchange and respect are key components.

:p What defines the collaborative role between a consultant and a manager?
??x
The collaborative role is characterized by active involvement from both parties in critical phases such as data collection, goal setting, action plan development, and shared responsibility for success or failure. It promotes an interdependent partnership where decision-making is bilateral, involving mutual exchange and respect for the expertise of each party.

#### Interdependence and Shared Responsibility
In this approach, consultants and managers work together to solve problems, ensuring that both are responsible for planning, implementation, and results. This shared responsibility leads to a more holistic problem-solving process.

??x
