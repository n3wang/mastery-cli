#### Case Questions
Background context: Following the initial assessment of your social skills and maturity, the second part of a consulting interview typically involves case questions. These questions are pivotal because they can either seal or destroy a candidate’s chances of landing the job.

:p What is the significance of case questions in a consulting interview?
??x
Case questions are significant because they test your problem-solving abilities, analytical skills, and ability to apply logic to real-world problems. While you may pass the initial social assessment, a poorly handled case question can quickly lead to rejection. Conversely, acing the case question but struggling with interpersonal skills could also cause issues.

These questions require clear thinking, structured approaches, and effective communication—skills that are essential in consulting roles.

Background context: The last part of the interview requires extensive research about both the industry and the company. This helps you prepare insightful questions and ensure that your understanding of the firm is up to date.

:p Why is conducting thorough research before an interview important?

??x
Thorough research before an interview is crucial because it allows you to understand the company's history, current projects, and future goals better. #### Structuring the Consulting Interview
Background context explaining the structure of the consulting interview process. The first round typically includes multiple interviews focused on case questions and analysis, followed by second and third rounds that are similar but more intensive.

The objective is to familiarize candidates with what to expect during each round so they can prepare adequately.
:p What does a typical first-round consulting interview consist of?
??x
A typical first-round consulting interview usually consists of multiple interviews focused on case questions and analysis. This round aims to evaluate the candidate's analytical skills, ability to think critically, and problem-solving abilities.

For example:
- The interviewer might present a case study and ask the candidate to analyze data and make recommendations.
- There may be group activities where candidates work together to solve problems.

Candidates should prepare by practicing similar case questions and analyzing charts.
