#### Distinguishing Between Business Goals, Strategies, and Performance Strategies
Strategies outline the overarching plan for achieving business goals, while performance strategies focus on enhancing employee performance. Understanding these distinctions helps in aligning discussions effectively.
:p How do you distinguish between a business goal, a business strategy, and a performance strategy?
??x
- **Business Goal**: An operational objective measured in numbers that represents what needs to be achieved. Example: "We need to grow market share of our two signature brands."
- **Business Strategy**: An overarching plan for accomplishing the business goal. Example: "We are acquiring a company that manufactures products that will complement the products already in our brands. We want to ensure the integration of that company is effectively done."
- **Performance Strategy**: A plan for enhancing employee performance at the individual or organizational level. Example: "We need to increase the flexibility of our workforce so they can operate in a cross-functional manner across our growing number of products."

By distinguishing these, you can ensure that discussions are structured and focused on the right aspects.
