#### Discovery Phase Overview
The discovery phase is a critical part of consulting where the main objectives are to develop an independent and fresh perspective on the client's situation and create a process that leads to commitment, ownership, and action. The traditional approach often focuses on diagnosing problems, whereas more recent methods emphasize possibilities and strengths.

:p What are the two primary purposes for the discovery phase?
??x
The two primary purposes for the discovery phase are: (1) to develop an independent and fresh way of looking at what is going on, and (2) to create a process that leads to client commitment, ownership, and action. The goal is not to be right but to be effective and powerful.

#### Positive Deviance Methodology
Positive Deviance is a methodology where consultants look for examples in systems where something is working well. By asking what makes those success stories unique, the method can lead to improvements in various contexts such as businesses, poor villages, and hospitals.

:p What is an example of applying the Positive Deviance approach?
??x
An example of applying the Positive Deviance approach is when consultants listen to examples of something that is working well. For instance, in villages where some children are healthier than others, they ask what those families do differently. This questioning can lead to improvements in child health and overall community well-being.

#### Appreciative Inquiry Methodology
Appreciative Inquiry focuses on what is working well for an organization, traditions worth sustaining, and people's visions for the future. This approach helps create a positive and forward-looking culture within organizations.

:p What is another name for the approach that looks at strengths instead of problems?

Another name for the approach that looks at strengths instead of problems is the asset-based or strength-based approach.
