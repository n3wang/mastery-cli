#### Creating a Platform for Openness and Doubt
In many organizational settings, open conversations are constrained. However, bringing more openness into these environments is crucial for honest dialogue about failure and uncertainty.

:p Why is creating an environment for open conversation important?
??x
Creating an environment where all voices can be heard openly is vital because it allows for a more truthful and comprehensive exchange of ideas. In traditional settings, conversations often remain private—occuring in restrooms, at water coolers, over meals or drinks. These informal discussions are not part of the formal organizational discourse, which can lead to an incomplete understanding of reality.

By bringing these private conversations into the open through structured assemblies where all perspectives are welcomed, organizations can foster a culture of transparency and honesty. This openness is essential for discussing failures and uncertainties candidly without fear of retribution or judgment.
