#### Performance Consulting Logic Overview
Performance consulting logic is based on identifying gaps between what SHOULD be occurring and what IS occurring. This gap analysis drives the development of initiatives aimed at closing these gaps through implemented solutions.

:p What does performance consulting logic focus on?
??x
Performance consulting logic focuses on identifying discrepancies between desired outcomes (SHOULD) and current realities (IS). Gaps are identified to inform the design and implementation of targeted interventions or initiatives.

#### Alignment and Measurement Model Structure
The Alignment and Measurement Model consists of several key elements: Assessment, Objectives, Results, and Performance Impact. It guides the process from identifying the current state ("IS") to setting goals ("SHOULD") and evaluating outcomes.

:p What are the main components of the Alignment and Measurement Model?
??x
The main components of the Alignment and Measurement Model include:
- **Assessment**: Identifies the current or "IS" state.
- **Objectives**: Defines what should be achieved, or "SHOULDs."
- **Results**: Compares intended outcomes with actual results.
- **Performance Impact**: Evaluates broader business impacts.

The model ensures alignment between these elements from initiation to completion of a performance consulting initiative.
