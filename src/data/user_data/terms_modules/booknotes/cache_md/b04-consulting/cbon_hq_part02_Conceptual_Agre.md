#### Developing Conceptual Agreement
Conceptual agreement is crucial as it involves aligning objectives, measures of success, and value before moving forward with any proposal or project.

:p What are the three aspects of conceptual agreement?
??x
The three aspects of conceptual agreement include:
1. **Objectives**: Business outcomes rather than inputs.
2. **Measures of Success (Metrics)**: Indicators to measure progress and completion.
3. **Value**: The worth of the project, demonstrating ROI with your fee.

Example questions for each aspect:

**Objectives**
- What is the ideal outcome you'd like to experience?
- What results are you trying to accomplish?

**Measures of Success (Metrics)**
- How will you know we've accomplished your intent?
- How, specifically, will the operation be different when we're done?

**Value**
- What will these results mean for your organization?

:p How do objectives differ from inputs in business outcomes?
??x
Objectives describe a component of an improved client condition and are about achieving business outcomes, never deliverables or inputs. For example, reducing closing time for the average sale is an outcome, while implementing training programs or focus groups are merely means to achieve that objective.

#### Establishing Measures of Success (Metrics)
Metrics are essential for evaluating progress and attributing success to specific contributions.

:p What types of metrics can be used?
??x
Metrics can be both objective (based on empirical evidence) and subjective (based on perceptions and observed behavior), as long as both you and the buyer agree on their reporting. Examples include:
- Sales reports showing rapidity of sales closing after first contact.
- Quarterly sales totals of client reorders and rapidity.
- Anecdotal reporting of stress levels at meetings and absentee reports submitted weekly.

:p How do you ensure effective measures are established?
??x
Ask questions like "How will you know we've accomplished your intent?" or "What indicators will you use to assess our progress?" This helps in aligning with the buyer's expectations and ensuring clear, measurable outcomes.

#### Determining Value of the Project
Value is crucial for demonstrating ROI and justifying fees. It involves understanding both business and personal impacts.

:p How do objectives and value relate?
??x
Objectives define what needs to be achieved (e.g., reducing closing time for average sales), while value quantifies the worth of achieving these outcomes. For example, improved margins on new sales are a direct financial benefit that can be articulated in terms of increased investment in R&D or larger investor dividends.

Understanding potential failures helps in risk management and contingency planning.
