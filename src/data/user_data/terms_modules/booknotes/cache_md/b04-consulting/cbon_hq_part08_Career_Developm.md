#### Succession Planning
Background context: A well-planned succession can ensure that top positions are filled by capable individuals even when key personnel leave the organization. It's a critical aspect of organizational health and stability.

:p What is an indicator of good succession planning?
??x
An indicator of good succession planning is:

- **People Who Leave Acquire Top Jobs Elsewhere:** When employees who depart from the organization go on to take top roles in other companies, it suggests that they were well-prepared for leadership positions within the company.

For example, if several managers or executives leave and later become CEOs or COOs of different firms, this could indicate a strong succession plan. Conversely, if these same individuals remain in mid-level positions or are replaced by external hires, it may suggest weak succession planning.
