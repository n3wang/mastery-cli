这些年来，我经历了很多。我一个人在美国生活和工作，一边上学一边工作，毕业后进入了摩根大通，从事工程咨询相关的工作。从外表来看，我的人生似乎还不错，收入在同龄人中也算稳定。

但我内心一直有一种不完整的感觉。

在工作中，我常常觉得自己更像是一份可以随时被替换的合同，而不是被长期培养的人。虽然薪资不错，但我很难建立真正的归属感。长期处在这样的环境里，让我精神上一直处于紧张状态，也慢慢影响了我的情绪和生活。

感觉很孤独，一直在考虑转行到医疗领域，做一名护士。
Gǎnjué hěn gūdú, yīzhí zài kǎolǜ zhuǎnháng dào yīliáo lǐngyù, zuò yī míng hùshì.

Over the years, I've been through a lot. I lived and worked alone in the United States, juggling studies and work, and after graduation, I joined JPMorgan Chase, working in engineering consulting. On the surface, my life seems pretty good, and my income is stable compared to my peers.

But deep down, I've always had a feeling of incompleteness. 

我甚至都没被邀请参加年度派对。 i wasn't even invited to the yearly party

At work, I often feel more like a contract employee who can be replaced at any time, rather than someone who is being nurtured long-term. Although the salary is good, I find it difficult to establish a true sense of belonging. Being in this environment for a long time keeps me in a constant state of tension, which has gradually affected my emotions and my life.

除此之外，我也对未来的职业发展感到一些不确定。随着人工智能的发展，计算机行业正在快速变化，这种不稳定感让我长期处于压力之中。我并不是逃避，而是意识到自己需要寻找一个更踏实、更有挑战性的方向。

我发现自己在领导力、自信心以及面对困难时的心理强度方面，还有很多不足。我希望在真正承担更大的家庭责任之前，先让自己变得更成熟、更有能力。也许这些想法听起来有点幼稚，但对我来说却是非常真实的感受。

其实，在大学时期，我就多次认真考虑过参军这条道路。但因为我知道妈妈会非常担心，所以我一次又一次地放弃了这个想法。可是，这个念头始终没有离开过我。无论我做什么选择，它都会回到我的心里。

Besides this, I also feel some uncertainty about my future career development. With the rapid development of artificial intelligence, the computer industry is changing rapidly, and this instability has kept me under constant pressure. I'm not running away, but I realize I need to find a more grounded and challenging direction.

More importantly, I've discovered that I still have many shortcomings in leadership, self-confidence, and mental fortitude when facing difficulties. I hope to become more mature and capable before truly taking on greater family responsibilities. These thoughts may sound naive, but they are very real feelings for me.

In fact, during my university years, I seriously considered joining the military many times. But because I knew my mother would worry a lot, I repeatedly gave up the idea. However, this thought has never left me. No matter what choice I make, it always comes back to me.

我并不是因为情绪冲动，也不是因为不满意现在的收入。我是希望通过军队的训练环境，让自己获得真正的成长，学习纪律、领导力、压力管理和实际技能。

我特别考虑成为海军医疗兵。这个岗位类似军队里的医护人员，可以接受系统的医疗训练，在真实环境中积累经验。未来如果条件允许，我也希望申请医学院。美国的医学体系是先完成本科学位，然后申请医学院。虽然我已经有学位，但还需要补修一些医学基础课程。我计划通过服役后获得的教育福利（GI Bill）来支付这些课程费用，而不是依靠家庭经济支持。

我今天和您谈这件事，不是想向您要钱。我知道自己已经是成年人，也应该为自己的选择负责。我只是希望听听您的看法，因为您的意见对我来说很重要。

I'm not doing this out of emotional impulse, nor because I'm dissatisfied with my current income. I hope to gain genuine growth through the military's training environment, learning discipline, leadership, stress management, and practical skills.

I'm particularly considering becoming a Navy medic. This role is similar to that of a medical officer in the military, offering systematic medical training and real-world experience. If circumstances allow, I also hope to apply to medical school in the future. The US medical system requires completing a bachelor's degree before applying to medical school. Although I already have a degree, I still need to take some foundational medical courses. I plan to use my GI Bill (educational benefits) after my service to pay for these courses, rather than relying on family financial support.

I'm not talking to you about this today to ask for money. I know I'm an adult and should be responsible for my choices. I just want to hear your opinion because it's important to me.
